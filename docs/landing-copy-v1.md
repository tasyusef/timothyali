## Editorial reasoning

The opening retains the previously selected positioning. The second beat draws on Timothy's description of being mellow outwardly and intensely invested in aesthetics and design, without claiming a new personal backstory. The third explains his breadth through involvement and ownership, followed by founding/shipping experience. The closing invites a conversation about the visitor's project.

The main narrative is 61 words by whitespace count, excluding the optional credit and actions (count corrected during the version 02 review). Its short passages leave room for the environment and can be split into smaller moments during storyboarding. No scene duration, scroll length, layout, or camera route is selected here.

Compared with the earlier examples, this draft removes “the details you feel before you notice” because it is abstract and polished beyond the voice in Timothy's messages. It gives motion an explicit place alongside brand, product, and front end, reflecting the established range.

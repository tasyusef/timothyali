## Observations (Claude, first read)
- Positioning is split three ways (UX designer / product-brand-motion-frontend / designer-builder). None of them is wrong, but a visitor gets a different pitch depending on where they land.
- The strongest verifiable proof is in crypto/XRPL and in founder roles. The site presents these as line items rather than as a story.
- "Designer who codes / uses AI" is now a crowded claim. The receipts (live products with revenue) are the differentiator, not the claim.

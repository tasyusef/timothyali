## Environment language to carry forward

Flat, angular silhouettes; strong yellow/black figure-ground relationships; long wedges and asymmetrical towers; depth expressed through overlap, perspective, scale, and occlusion; large open areas for type; a small human figure that makes the city feel enormous. The sampled frames also show softened/darkened edges and yellow variation: the image is more atmospheric than two perfectly uniform vector fills. This observation does not choose the site's final texture or exact yellow.

## Proposed translation to scroll — interpretation, not a decision

Let scroll advance the visitor through spatial changes: an opening field, entry into the skyline, movement toward the road's vanishing point, and a more enclosed radial space that reveals a focal point. The actual portfolio beats, copy, number of scenes, and camera route still need a storyboard.

The strongest transferable idea is the way the same graphic vocabulary changes its relationship to the viewer. Buildings can establish a skyline, pass close enough to occlude the view, and then frame the next space. That gives the journey continuity.

My recommendation is to let selected work and short copy occupy deliberate pauses/openings within that journey. The film's credits demonstrate how little type a frame needs. Exact placement of projects, wordmark, and contact remains a proposal. Jacquard 24 is still the selected identity face; the film's condensed type does not replace it.

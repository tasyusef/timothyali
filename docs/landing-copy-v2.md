# Landing copy — version 02 proposal

2026-09-08. Proposed tightening in response to Timothy's feedback on storyboard 01. Version 01 remains the accepted wording until this proposal is approved. Decision 0026 accepts the new typographic direction, not these exact edits. Natural case below is source copy; the storyboard uses uppercase heavy display type and selected lowercase Jacquard words as presentation choices.

## Proposed passages

1. I’m tim. Designer for teams that don’t have one yet.
2. I get invested in what I make.
3. How they look. How they move. How they work. — *Superseded 2026-09-09 by decision 0050: “How it looks. moves. works.”*
4. From the first idea to something people use. Brand. Product. Motion. Front end.
5. I’ve cofounded products. Helped teams ship theirs.
6. Tell me what you’re building.

Actions remain: **see my work** and **get in touch**.

## What changed and why

- Opening: words retained; casing becomes flexible.
- Investment: removed “pretty” and condensed “the things” to “what”. This is more direct; it loses a little of v1's conversational softness and should be judged in the new display treatment.
- Three qualities: words retained, with capitalization used for visual weight.
- Ownership: condensed the long sentence into a short statement spanning idea to use. The preceding first-person passages establish the speaker. This is the largest cut.
- Experience: two short statements retain the cofounder claim and helping teams ship, with fewer connective words.
- Invitation: words retained. “building” gets proposed Jacquard emphasis.

The shortened copy keeps the four accepted narrative beats. It does not introduce new claims about clients, revenue, results, or work history.

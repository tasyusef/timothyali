# Design audit, round 2: fable (PX-128, 2026-10-28)

Verdict first: **Needs changes.** No HIGH findings. Three MEDIUM (keyboard focus lands under the sticky chrome on Shift+Tab, state cues vanish under forced colours, reading paragraphs on the Toolbox pages set at 27/32) and six LOW.

## Scope and Coverage

- **Mode:** `full` (better-interface), all six domain skills loaded with their reference files.
- **Scope:** every public page on `http://127.0.0.1:4173` (`pnpm preview` build): `/`, `/work/`, the seven studies, `/toolbox/`, the four tool pages, `/toolbox/agents/`, `/contact/`, `/nope/` (404). Out of scope: `/admin/`, `/og/`, the résumé PDF.
- **Stack:** SvelteKit 2 / Svelte 5, plain CSS in `src/lib/styles/*.css` (primitives, tokens, base, type, chrome, layout, blocks) plus route-scoped styles; bitmap faces on the cell table at the top of `src/app.css`; 8px spacing units; `data-theme` on `<html>`; motion via `.site.motion`.
- **How:** my own Playwright Chrome (`chromium.launch({ channel: 'chrome' })`), scripts in this folder (`static.mjs`, `nav.mjs`, `field.mjs`, `keyboard.mjs`, `forms.mjs`, `followup.mjs`, `followup2.mjs`), captures in `shots/`, logs and JSON in `data/`. Widths 1440, 1100, 900, 700, 420, 390, 320; dark and light; motion on, motion off and `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`; touch emulation at 390; forced colours and `prefers-contrast: more` emulated; 200% zoom as 720 CSS px at DPR 2.
- **Boundary:** the contact relay was intercepted (`page.route('**/api/contact**')`), downloads were intercepted (`**/download/**`), the share sheet cannot open in headless Chrome (the clipboard fallbacks were exercised by removing `navigator.share`). No screen reader was run; names and roles were read from the DOM and computed styles.

## Verification

Run from the project root; the preview on 4173 was already up and was not touched. Full logs: `data/static.json`, `data/nav.log`, `data/field.log`, `data/keyboard.log`, `data/forms.log`, `data/followup2.log`.

**Passed**

- `node docs/iterations/pixel-v2/128-design-audit-2/agents/fable/static.mjs`: 17 pages × 7 widths (dark) + 1440 and 390 in light = 153 captures. No horizontal overflow anywhere (320 included). One `h1` per page, nested `h2`/`h3`, `main`/`nav[Main navigation]`/`header`/`footer` landmarks, `lang="en"`, viewport without `maximum-scale`. Every `img` has `alt` (PARC: 32 of 32, descriptive). Every rendered size is on the cell table except the 1.5-cell fit step (0204) and an `sr-only` heading. Text contrast: every visible pair passes WCAG AA and APCA Lc 60 in both themes except the disabled demo step (#7). Only console noise: the analytics beacon `/api/e` answers 403 on the preview (no Vercel env), and the 404 page's own 404.
- `nav.mjs`: all four nav links and the wordmark navigate with `aria-current` set; the readout path decodes ("~/tim/work"); the seven Work cards open their studies; the Next chain runs parc → jade → firstledger → xrpcafe → firststrike → do-androids-dream → toolbox → parc; the study page foot goes to Contact; Home's card, "All work", "Selected work" (scrolls to `#work`, y=864) and the résumé link work; the Toolbox CTA scrolls to `#tools` (picker top 64 under the 32px strip); the four picker buttons set `aria-pressed`, swap the story and the guide link; each tool page's "All four tools" returns to `/toolbox/`, its Next and "Get it" links resolve; "CLI & MCP docs" and the eight jump links work (sections land at 32px, below the collapsed strip); "Lockup options" lands on `/toolbox/lockup/#cli` at the chrome's edge; the 404's "Back to home" works. Settings cog at 1440 and 390: `aria-label="Settings"`, `aria-expanded`, menu opens over the strip, Theme flips `data-theme`, Motion flips `data-motion` and `aria-pressed`, both hold across a client navigation and (in `followup.mjs`, with no init script re-seeding storage) across a full load of `/work/` with `data-theme="light"` painted before hydration; Escape closes and refocuses the cog; a click outside closes; Enter and Space toggle it. Hover and press: 20 controls change on hover and go to the accent on press (cards change their mat and arrow, verified in `followup2.mjs`). Scroll at 1440 and 390 over seven pages: CLS 0.000 to 0.003, the header collapses on the way down and returns on the way up, the study's point text holds while it fits.
- `field.mjs` (six fields: Home, PARC, Jade, Toolbox, Contact, 404): the field crosshair cursor; hover moves the canvas, a click rings, a drag moves it, a 1.5s hold charges, release fires; after the drag no text is selected (length 0) and nothing has focus; a drag across the heading and the first link selects nothing; a drag that starts on the heading still selects its text; links click afterwards (Home, Jade, Toolbox, Contact, 404 navigated; PARC's live link was below the fold in that run, not a failure). With `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` and with Motion [off]: hover, drag, hold and release change nothing, no cursor blinks, headings show their final text. Touch at 390 (pointer: coarse): a swipe in the field scrolls the page (0→345, 358, 641), selects nothing; a tap rings.
- `keyboard.mjs`: on every page the first Tab is the visible "Skip to content" (y=8, 32px tall); Enter moves focus to `main`. Every focused control shows a `solid 2px` ring in `--accent-text` with 2px offset: yellow on the dark ground, deep yellow `#6f6200` on the light ground and on the white panel (inputs, Send, the panel's links, the settings rows). No stop is inside `aria-hidden`. Space and Enter work on the picker, the demo switches (`role="switch"`), chips, tree rows, Continue (focus moves to the review heading) and Back (focus returns to Continue), the slider (ArrowRight 10→15), a Work card (Enter navigates), the clip button (Space pauses and renames itself), the options `<details>` at 390.

# Design audit of timothyali.com (PX-125), reviewer: fable

Method: `better-interface` in **full** mode, with the six domain skills (accessibility, layout, writing, typography, colors, ui) and their reference files loaded. Project rules read first: `AGENTS.md`, `docs/design-system.md`, the decision log through 0227. Read-only: nothing outside this folder was changed.

## Scope and Coverage

- **Mode:** full. **Scope:** the public site served at `http://127.0.0.1:4173` (the built preview), all seventeen URLs in the brief (`/`, `/work/`, seven studies, `/toolbox/`, four tool pages, `/toolbox/agents/`, `/contact/`, `/nope/`). Out of scope: `/admin/`, `/og/`, the résumé PDF.
- **Stack:** SvelteKit 2 / Svelte 5, plain CSS in `src/lib/styles/` (primitives, tokens, base, type, chrome, layout, blocks), plus route-scoped `<style>` blocks and `src/lib/components/toolbox/demo.css`. Hex colour tokens, 8px spacing tokens, font sizes literal on the cell table. Breakpoints 420 / 700 / 900 / 1100.
- **States inspected:** dark and light theme; motion on, motion off and `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`; keyboard focus on every page; the contact form empty, invalid, sending, sent, failed and rate-limited (relay mocked with `page.route`); widths 1440, 1100, 900, 720 (1440 at 200 %), 700, 420, 390, 320. Phone widths ran as a touch device (`isMobile`, `hasTouch`) so `pointer: coarse` rules applied.
- **Interaction pass (brief addendum):** every nav link, the wordmark, all seven Work cards, a study's Next and foot links, Home's card / All work / Get in touch / Selected work chip, the Toolbox hero CTA, all four picker + guide links, tool-page Next and back, CLI links and the jump list, the 404's way home; the cog and both settings, by click and by Space / Enter / Escape, and persistence across five pages and a client-side navigation; hover and press on ten kinds of control; the pointer swell and click ripples with motion on and off; keyboard Enter / Space on links, picker buttons, demo switches, chips, tree rows, the `<details>` summary and the clip button; the eight download links with `**/download/**` intercepted; the phone "Send the link" control; wheel-scrolling every page to the bottom and back at 1440 and 390.
- **Boundary:** no real screen reader was run; the accessibility tree was read from the DOM. Nothing was sent through the real relay and no file was downloaded.

## Verification

Scripts and data live in this folder: `shots.mjs` writes `sweep.json` and `shots/`; `checks.mjs` writes `checks.json`; `interact.mjs` writes `interact.json` (section 1 and 2 output is in `interact-part1.log`, the rest in `interact.log`); `probe2.mjs`; `contrast.mjs`. All ran against `http://127.0.0.1:4173` with Chrome (`chromium.launch({ channel: 'chrome' })`).

**Passed**

- `node shots.mjs`: 174 captures; horizontal overflow only on `/toolbox/` at 701 to 750 (#3) and `/toolbox/specimen/` at ≤328 (#4). Every page clear at 320, 390, 420, 700, 900, 1100 and 1440, and at 720 otherwise. Page titles are unique and specific on all 17 pages.
- `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` with no saved choice: `data-motion="off"`, cursors `animation: none`, every `Decode` shows its full text, both clips paused with a visible Play button, the field pixel-identical 1.2 s apart on Home, PARC and Toolbox.
- Motion off by the setting: the field did not change under pointer movement or a click; the press still lit "Get in touch" to the accent (0099 holds with motion off).
- Motion on: the hero typed, the pointer swell changed the field, a click added ripples, a double-click beside the copy selected nothing.
- Keyboard: on all five pages the skip link is the first stop, Enter on it moves focus to `#main` and the next Tab lands on the first control inside main; every stop matched `:focus-visible` and showed the 2px ring on the page ground (the panel and menu cases are #1). Enter on nav links and cards navigated; Space on the picker, the demo switch, a chip, a tree row and the `<details>` summary toggled them; Escape closed the settings menu and returned focus to the cog; Space on the cog, Motion and Theme toggled each (Theme flipped `data-theme` and the `theme-color` meta).
- Settings persistence: Motion off plus Theme light held on `/work/`, `/work/parc/`, `/toolbox/`, `/contact/` and `/nope/` and across a client-side navigation; the cog stays hidden until the saved choices are read.
- Navigation: all four nav links set `aria-current="page"` on their page; the wordmark goes home; all seven cards open their study; Next chains study to study and tool to tool; foot links reach `/contact/` and `/toolbox/`; the four guide links, "CLI & MCP setup", the option links (`/toolbox/lockup/#cli` landed with the heading 96px below the top), the jump list (sections landed 32 to 62px from the top; "Organize" at 214 because the page ends), the hero chips (`#work` at 32px, `#tools` at 64px) and the 404's "Back to home" all worked.
- Hover and press: nav links, the hero chip, quiet links (the press draws the cell block), cards (mat dither and arrow colour), rows, the Toolbox CTA (hover nudges 8px with motion on), the picker, Send and the panel's quiet links all changed on hover and again on press.
- Contact form (relay mocked): an empty submit marked all three fields `aria-invalid` with `aria-describedby` notes and focused Name; a bad email focused Email with "That doesn't look like an email address"; typing cleared the note; sending disabled the button with the label "Sending"; the sent state cleared the form and announced through the polite `#contact-status`; the failed state kept the typed message and re-enabled Send; `?error=rate-limited` rendered its note. Inputs are 27px (no iOS zoom), labels wrap their inputs, `autocomplete` is set. The block caret sits at the insertion point.
- Downloads: all eight links requested `/download/v1.2.3/<file>?s=Direct+%2F+unknown` (intercepted, not downloaded); the releases link goes to GitHub.
- Scrolling every page at 1440 and 390: the header collapses on the way down and returns on the way up (a 300px wheel up, given time for the smooth scroll, brought it back on every page tried); the study's point text holds sticky under the chrome at 1440; no page changed height while scrolling.
- Contrast (`contrast.mjs`): fg on paper 15.4:1; accent-text 12.2:1 (dark) and 5.3:1 (light); on-accent 12.2:1; dim 5.4:1 (dark) and 5.2:1 (light); panel dim 5.2:1 / 5.4:1.
- Headings: one `h1` per page, levels nest (h1, h2, h3 on Home and Toolbox), every heading size descends with level; all images carry `alt`; landmarks: one `main`, labelled `nav`s, labelled groups; no unnamed control on any page.
- Target sizes: at touch widths every link and button measured 40px or taller through the `::after` reach (0206); the desktop 16px text links sit under the spacing exception (8px row gaps, 24px column gaps).

**Not verified**

- A real screen reader (VoiceOver, NVDA): announcement order and the live regions were read from attributes only.
- A real phone's share sheet (#12), real downloads and the live relay.
- Windows forced-colors mode and Firefox's scrollbar colours.
- The dither wipes at 10 % speed; they were observed at full speed only.

## Verdict

`Block`: one HIGH finding remains (#1, the focus ring on inverted grounds).

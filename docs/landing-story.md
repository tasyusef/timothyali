# Landing story

Status: narrative structure accepted on 2026-09-08, decision 0023. Landing copy v1 is accepted in 0024; the storyboard remains in development.

Accepted copy: `landing-copy-v1.md` (version 01, decision 0024). The example wording below is retained as the earlier proposal.

## Purpose

Introduce tim and connect his personality and taste to what he can bring to a team. A visitor should understand who he is, what he cares about, what he could own, and why they want to see his work.

## Site structure

Home contains the animated introduction. Work contains the project index and individual case studies. Contact is the other main destination; whether it is a page, section, form, or direct link is not settled. Work is reachable immediately through navigation. About is optional.

## Accepted story arc

| Beat | What the visitor learns | Content direction |
|---|---|---|
| Meet tim | Who he is and who he works with | Timothy Ali wordmark; personal tim voice; designer for teams without a designer. |
| What he cares about | His taste and attention to the whole experience | The relationship between how something looks, feels, and works; identity, product, and detail. |
| How he works and why to believe him | What he can own and the experience behind it | Brand, product, motion, front end; founding and shipping experience; concise supporting proof. |
| Where to go next | How to explore further or start a conversation | Explore Work and make contact. |

Emotional progression: curiosity → connection → confidence → a next step.

How to read this sheet

Judge each at the big size first, then at the small line, then flipped. A wordmark has to survive all three. The rule from 0018: blackletter is rationed to the wordmark and a few display moments; the working type is a Swiss grotesk and will be chosen separately. Adobe Fonts availability of the three named faces is from a 2026 round-up and should be confirmed in the Creative Cloud app.

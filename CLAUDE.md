## How to write to me

(Mirrors my global `~/.claude/CLAUDE.md` so cloud sessions and other machines get it too.)

I have ADHD. Long, dense replies are hard for me to follow. Write every response so it can be scanned and understood fast.

### Layout
- Lead with the answer or the outcome in one line. Details come after.
- Short sections with clear headings when a reply covers more than one thing.
- Bullets over paragraphs. One idea per bullet. Keep bullets short.
- Bold the key word or decision in a line so it stands out.
- Keep paragraphs to two or three sentences at most.
- Put what you need from me last, on its own, as a short question or numbered options.
- When I have choices, number them and mark your recommendation.

### Language
- Plain English by default.
- Use precise technical terms where they are the clearest way to say it (file names, APIs, CSS properties, commands). Don't dumb them down, but don't pile them up either.
- No filler, no recap of what I just said, no long caveats.

### Length
- Say less. If something is optional detail, leave it out or offer it in one line.
- Don't list everything you checked; say what passed and anything that didn't.
